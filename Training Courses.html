Training Courses


Strain Engineering provides train course for employees and board member of companies. Companies are able to apply for course, what content to be covered and how long they want their training to last. Strain takes four to five training each year depending on how companies respond to our training adverts.

This ongoing year we are project to hold at least three to six training in all the quarters.

Each year, Strain holds an induction training for board members for UMSCC that part-takes between February and April.
