Refrescho Water & Beverages


Refreshco produces water and beverages with the brand name Manna Brook. The product line includes: 500ml, 2liter and 5liter bottled water. The bottled water is packaged in different sizes according to the product. The packages are as follows:

  • 500ml bottled water is sold in a pack of 12 units
  • 2ltr is sold in a pack of 6 units
  • 5ltr is sold in a pack 4 unit

If you want a customised packaging, it can be done for you.
